Sashay’s Shimmy Shimmy Ko-Ko Bop
================================
DJ: sashay delmonico
Category: Freeform
Schedule: Every week on Thu at 8:00 PM
Link: https://spinitron.com/WCBN/show/248591/Sashay-s-Shimmy-Shimmy-Ko-Ko-Bop

WCBN's "sashay's shimmy shimmy ko ko bop" is my freeform show that has a different vibe from one week to the next. I started doing radio at WCBN in 1990, taking a break for 10 or so years when I was immersed in my job as director of the Ann Arbor Film Festival and then recovering from breast cancer treatment in the early 2000s. After co-hosting "vick and mick's tossed salad" with DJ Mick for 7 years, I started shimmy shimmy ko ko bop, using the name sashay delmonico as my radio identity. The name was given to me by Hot Rize/Red Knuckles and the Trailblazers bass player, Charles Sawtelle, as my dance partner, Jim Kruz, and I were coming out on stage to jitterbug in front of the band at Meadowbrook Theater.

I can't say that I love all kinds of music, but the music I know and love is what I enjoy sharing on my show: folk, 70s rock, classical, blues, early country, bluegrass, Italian, French, good life, Cuban, N'awlins, jazz. I mix it up or sometimes go heavy within a genre, but guaranteed what you heard last week will be different next week.

What really and truly rocks my world is a perfect segue and the surprise element of switching from one moment to the next with a different sound and feeling. I listen to radio stations and DJs around the country on the WWW, hoping for that surprise element that makes freeform radio so special. It's an honor to be part of the WCBN community and to be a contributor to keeping radio alive.
